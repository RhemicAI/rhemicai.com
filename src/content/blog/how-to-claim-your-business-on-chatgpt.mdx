ChatGPT now has business profiles.

On November 10, 2026, OpenAI launched **Businesses in ChatGPT** in beta. A business can claim its profile, verify it, and control the facts ChatGPT shows about it. It is the Google Business Profile moment for AI.

If your customers ask ChatGPT who to call, this is the first thing to do this week.

## What a ChatGPT business profile is

When someone asks ChatGPT about a business, or asks it to recommend one, ChatGPT can show a business profile. The profile holds your name, what you do, where you are and how to reach you.

Until now, ChatGPT filled that in from whatever it found online. With a claimed profile, you control those facts.

You manage the profile in **Business Hub**. Business Hub also gives you insights: 28 days of website clicks from ChatGPT, how often your business is mentioned, and the topics people ask about.

## How to claim your business on ChatGPT

The beta flow has four steps:

1. **Open Business Hub** and sign in with the ChatGPT account that will manage the listing. One account holds administrator access for each business page.
2. **Find your business.** Local stores, restaurants and service businesses claim an existing U.S. listing. Online businesses can add a new one.
3. **Verify the business.** OpenAI checks ownership through your business phone number, email, website domain or Shopify store.
4. **Pass the identity check.** The person who manages the listing verifies their identity with Persona. The verified badge appears after this step.

Shopify merchants can also connect their store from the Shopify admin.

The whole claim takes minutes. Because the identity check is personal, the owner or the manager of record has to do that part.

## Claiming is free. Getting recommended is the work.

OpenAI is clear on one point: a claimed profile carries no guarantee of better placement or more sales.

ChatGPT still decides who to recommend from everything it reads about you:

- **Your website.** Clear service pages, service areas and structured data that ChatGPT can quote.
- **Your Google Business Profile.** Complete categories, services, hours and photos.
- **Your reviews.** A steady flow of recent reviews, with responses.
- **What other sites say.** Directories, local roundups and citations that name you.

The claimed profile makes your facts correct. Those four things decide whether ChatGPT names you or your competitor.

Your competitor can claim their profile today too. The businesses that claim early and keep the rest of their presence strong get the head start.

## What to do this week

- Claim and verify your ChatGPT business profile.
- Check that your name, address, phone and hours match your Google Business Profile exactly.
- Ask ChatGPT the three questions your customers ask, such as "best plumber near me". Write down who it names.
- Fix the gaps on your website and Google Business Profile first. Those are the pages ChatGPT reads.
